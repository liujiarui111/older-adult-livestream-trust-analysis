# Confidential Survey and PLS-SEM Research Project

A first-author research project demonstrating an end-to-end quantitative research workflow while preserving the confidentiality of an unpublished manuscript.

## Confidentiality statement

The manuscript is under revision and has not been submitted. To protect the originality and confidentiality of the work, this public portfolio does **not** disclose:

- the manuscript title or abstract;
- the research topic, question, hypotheses, or theoretical framework;
- questionnaire items, construct names, or measurement instruments;
- sample size, participant characteristics, or collection channels;
- model diagrams, coefficients, statistical tables, findings, or conclusions;
- manuscript text, references, target journal, or coauthor information; or
- raw, cleaned, or respondent-level data.

## Research workflow

My work covered the complete quantitative-research pipeline:

1. Translate a research question into measurable constructs.
2. Develop and refine a structured questionnaire.
3. Establish response-quality screening rules before analysis.
4. Clean, code, and document survey data.
5. Assess the reflective measurement model.
6. Evaluate the structural model through bootstrapping.
7. Review explanatory power, predictive relevance, effect sizes, and collinearity.
8. Translate statistical results into a first-author academic manuscript.
9. Revise the analysis and writing in response to research-team feedback.

## Methods and tools

- **Excel:** response screening, coding, cleaning, and quality-control review
- **SPSS:** descriptive statistics and supporting diagnostic checks
- **SmartPLS 4:** PLS-SEM measurement and structural-model assessment
- **Research writing:** methods, results interpretation, limitations, and manuscript revision

## Model-assessment capabilities

The project demonstrates practical experience evaluating:

- indicator reliability through outer loadings;
- internal consistency using Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability;
- convergent validity using average variance extracted;
- discriminant validity using Fornell-Larcker and HTMT;
- collinearity using variance inflation factors;
- path significance through bootstrapping;
- explanatory power using R-squared;
- predictive relevance using Q-squared; and
- practical contribution using effect sizes.

## Author contribution

As first author, I participated across questionnaire development, response-quality screening, data cleaning and coding, PLS-SEM analysis, interpretation, manuscript drafting, and revision.
