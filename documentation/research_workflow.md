# Confidential research workflow

This document describes technical capabilities without revealing the manuscript's content.

## Survey preparation

- Operationalized conceptual variables using structured questionnaire items.
- Reviewed item wording, response scales, and coding consistency.
- Defined response-quality checks before model estimation.

## Data preparation

- Screened responses against documented quality rules.
- Recoded demographic and scale variables for analysis.
- Checked missing values, invalid categories, and inconsistent records.
- Maintained a clear boundary between raw and analytical datasets.

## Measurement-model assessment

- Reviewed outer loadings for indicator reliability.
- Assessed Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability.
- Evaluated average variance extracted for convergent validity.
- Checked Fornell-Larcker and HTMT for discriminant validity.

## Structural-model assessment

- Reviewed VIF statistics for collinearity.
- Interpreted bootstrapped path estimates and significance.
- Evaluated R-squared, Q-squared, and effect sizes.
- Distinguished statistical significance from substantive interpretation.

## Research communication

- Converted technical outputs into concise methods and results sections.
- Documented limitations and avoided causal claims unsupported by the design.
- Revised the manuscript while preserving consistency between data, tables, and narrative.

No study-specific constructs, hypotheses, statistics, or conclusions are disclosed here.
